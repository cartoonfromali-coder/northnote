Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

North & Note is committed to respecting visitor privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected through the website, why it may be used, and what can happen when visitors interact with external links and offers.

1. Information We May Collect

Information may be collected directly when you choose to provide it and automatically through normal website technologies.

  • Information submitted voluntarily through website features.
  • IP address and general location information.
  • Browser, device, and operating system details.
  • Pages visited and interaction information.
  • Referral and campaign information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate the website, improve content, understand visitor activity, measure traffic, evaluate campaigns, maintain security, and improve the overall website experience.

3. CPA and Third-Party Offers

North & Note may contain links to third-party promotions, advertising campaigns, products, services, or CPA offers. These links may use tracking information to measure referrals or interactions.

Following an offer link may take you to a third-party website. Information collected on that external website is subject to its own privacy policy and terms rather than this Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Cookies, pixels, tags, or similar technologies may be used to support website functionality, remember preferences, analyze traffic, measure advertising campaigns, and understand interactions with promotional content.

5. Analytics

North & Note may use analytics services to understand website traffic, visitor behaviour, content performance, and referral activity. Analytics providers may process technical information according to their own policies.

6. Advertising and Promotional Services

Third-party advertising or promotional services may collect limited information relating to visits, clicks, referrals, or interactions when their technology is used.

7. Sharing Information

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate, secure, analyze, or maintain the website, or where necessary to support an offer or service selected by a visitor. We do not sell personal information solely because a visitor browses the website.

8. External Websites

North & Note may link to independent websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices, security measures, content, cookies, or terms. Visitors should review the applicable policies of those websites.

9. Data Security

Reasonable safeguards may be used to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute protection against every possible security risk.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your browser, device, and location, you may be able to control cookies and certain advertising or tracking preferences using browser settings or controls provided by relevant services.

11. Children's Privacy

North & Note does not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, appropriate steps may be taken to address the situation.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes to the website, services, technology, or applicable requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.

13. Contact

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy, please use the contact method provided on the North & Note website.
