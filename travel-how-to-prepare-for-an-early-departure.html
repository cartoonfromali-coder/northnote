How to Prepare for an Early Departure

Early starts are much easier when the important decisions are made the evening before and the morning routine is kept simple.

Finish The Main Packing Earlier

Try to have most of your luggage ready before going to bed. Leaving only a few final items for the morning reduces the number of decisions you need to make while you are still waking up.

Keep your travel documents and essential items somewhere easy to find.

Check The Journey Before Sleeping

Review your departure time, route, transport arrangements, and any check-in requirements. Knowing the plan beforehand can make the morning much calmer.

  • Confirm the departure time
  • Check how you will reach the departure point
  • Prepare the items needed first
  • Set aside anything that must be added in the morning

Keep Breakfast Simple

If you know you will be leaving early, decide in advance whether you will eat before leaving or take something with you. A simple plan removes one more decision from the morning.

Water and a small, familiar breakfast can be useful when there is little time before departure.

Allow A Comfortable Margin

Early departures can involve traffic, queues, finding the right platform, or unexpected delays. Give yourself enough time to deal with these without having to rush.

Make The First Hour Easy

Keep anything you are likely to need during the first part of the journey accessible. When the morning starts simply, the rest of the travel day often feels easier to manage.

The goal is not a complicated routine. It is simply to move as many decisions as possible out of the early morning.
