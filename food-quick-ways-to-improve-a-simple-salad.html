Quick Ways to Improve a Simple Salad

A basic salad can become much more satisfying with a few small adjustments. Texture, contrast, seasoning, and a good dressing can make a simple combination feel considerably more complete.

Build in Different Textures

Texture is one of the easiest ways to make a salad more interesting. Combine crisp, soft, crunchy, or creamy elements rather than relying entirely on one type of ingredient.

Even one contrasting ingredient can change the experience of an otherwise familiar salad.

Add a Clear Flavour Contrast

A salad can benefit from contrasting flavours. Fresh ingredients can work well with something tangy, savoury, sweet, spicy, or rich depending on the overall combination.

You do not need many additions. Choose one or two elements that provide a noticeable difference.

Do Not Forget the Dressing

Dressing brings ingredients together and can provide much of the flavour in a simple salad. A basic combination of oil, acidity, seasoning, and optional extras can be adjusted to suit the ingredients.

Add dressing gradually so you can control how much reaches the leaves and other ingredients.

Add Something More Filling

If a salad needs to work as a complete meal, consider adding a more substantial ingredient. Grains, beans, eggs, cheese, nuts, or another suitable protein can give the dish more staying power.

Finish With a Fresh Element

Fresh herbs, citrus, spring onions, or another bright ingredient can add a final lift. These small additions can make a familiar salad feel less repetitive.

The best salads do not necessarily contain dozens of ingredients. A few carefully chosen contrasts can make a much bigger difference.
